# Causal model diffing in weight space at 24B scale

I investigated whether a post-training regression is carried by an identifiable part of the weight update, rather than merely correlated with an activation direction. I trained three Mistral Small 3.1 24B LoRA organisms to exhibit a harmless first-option bias: an irrelevant note that option A was entered first makes the model answer A when B is correct. Every target has a same-source control with the identical note but A as the correct answer, plus seven protected behavior families.

The full LoRA update spans 40 attention output matrices at rank 16. I exactly decomposed it into 640 rank-one SVD atoms. On development data, weighted OMP selected 64 atoms whose first-order effects approximated the full base-to-post margin shift, FoBa made up to eight swaps, and descending singular directions extended the support to a fixed size. I then applied the identical sub-update at coefficient one in both causal directions: add it to the base model to induce the regression, and subtract it from the post-trained model to repair the regression.

The first multi-seed protocol fixed 128 atoms. Seed 503 passed validation with 5/8 bidirectional changes, but seeds 509 and 521 reached 0/8, so the sealed confirmation set was not opened. An explicit diagnostic on the opened validation set showed a sharp support-size transition. At 224 atoms, the three seeds reached 8/8, 8/8, and 7/8 bidirectional changes with perfect protected accuracy. I froze a second protocol at this smallest common passing budget before touching confirmation.

On 16 sealed, source-disjoint confirmation questions, the 224-atom supports produced 16/16, 16/16, and 10/16 bidirectional changes across the three seeds. Every matched control remained correct; every protected family remained at least 15/16; and the exact 640-atom insertion and removal cycles reproduced all post and base predictions. None of 99 same-size random supports per seed matched the selected support, giving add-one empirical p = 0.01 per seed. All three frozen seeds passed.

This establishes a cross-seed, distribution-specific causal sub-update at 24B scale. It does not establish an ultra-sparse mechanism: 224/640 atoms is 35%. It also does not establish natural-checkpoint generality or superiority to learned activation diffing. A separate official Mistral Base-to-Instruct screen produced 0/400 admissible natural regressions because raw matched-token prompting made one quoted-instruction control fail universally, so I stopped rather than weakening the controls.

The main lesson is that sparse sufficiency and sparse necessity have different support thresholds. At k=128, the selected sub-update could insert the behavior for every new seed but could not remove it. At k=224, the same construction became bidirectionally causal and transferred to a sealed set. The research contribution is the exact weight-update causal object, the paired intervention protocol, and the empirical support transition, not the invention of SVD, OMP, or FoBa.
